    TI507N & TI507NB - Introduction to Linux¶

    Description¶

    This course is an introduction to operating system concepts, with a focus on GNU/Linux-based operating systems. You will discover the philosophy, internals, core components and powerful tools available on GNU/Linux systems through one of its distributions: Debian.

    This year the module coordinator is Mr. Yvan GUIFO. You can reach him at yvan.guifo-fodjo@efrei.fr.

    Schedule¶

    This module spans 14 hours of integrated Lecture-Lab sessions.

    • Week 36: 3 hours of practical sessions.
    • Week 37: 3 hours of practical sessions.
    • Week 38: 3 hours of practical sessions.
    • Week 39: 3 hours of practical work.
    • Week 40: 2 hours of practical work, including 1 hour of assessment (marked practical work) accounting for 40 per cent of the final mark.
    • Week 41: 1 hour final examination (DE) accounting for 60 per cent of the final mark.

    🎓 Labs

    🚀 Lab 1 — First commands File tree navigation, file manipulation, online help, wildcards. Beginner 🔒 Lab 2 — File system and permissions Inodes, links, rwx permissions, chmod, chown, umask, special bits. Intermediate ⚙️ Lab 3 — Working environment and C Compiler Environment variables, PATH, aliases, shell scripts, compiling with gcc. Intermediate ⚡ Lab 4 — Redirections, processes and signals stdin/stdout/stderr channels, pipes, processes, jobs, Unix signals. Advanced ⭐ Additional commands Extra exercises to deepen and consolidate your knowledge. Advanced
